# VocalNova

### From Fixed Exercises to Adaptive Speech Practice

VocalNova is an AI-powered personalized speech-practice application designed to make children's home speech practice more adaptive, engaging, and continuous.

Instead of following the same fixed sequence of exercises, VocalNova uses speech performance, engagement signals, learner history, and child-specific information to influence what the child practices next.

> **Speak. Practice. Adapt.**

---

## 📌 Problem

The challenge in speech practice is not only providing exercises. The bigger challenge is maintaining consistent and personalized practice between therapy sessions.

Common challenges include:

- Practice being postponed or skipped
- One-size-fits-all exercise sequences
- Limited feedback after an attempt
- Lack of continuity between practice sessions
- Difficulty deciding what the child should practice next

VocalNova focuses on the gap between therapy sessions by providing an adaptive speech-practice experience.

---

## 💡 Solution

VocalNova follows a continuous adaptive loop:

```text
Teach
  ↓
Listen
